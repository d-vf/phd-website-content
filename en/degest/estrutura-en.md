## Curricular Structure
 
The Doctoral Programme in Engineering and Management consists of a doctoral curricular component and the preparation of an original research thesis.
 
The overall structure corresponds to **240 ECTS**, organised as follows:
 
| Component | Credits |
|---|---:|
| Curricular Component / Common Core | 30 ECTS |
| Doctoral Thesis in Engineering and Management | 210 ECTS |
| **Total** | **240 ECTS** |
 
Current official information on the curriculum should be checked on Fénix, DGES, and the official accreditation and publication records.
 
## Organisation
 
The programme is organised into two main components:
 
1. **Curricular component**, corresponding to the Common Core, with **30 ECTS**.
2. **Research component**, corresponding to the curricular unit **Doctoral Thesis in Engineering and Management**, with **210 ECTS**.
The curricular component allows the student to consolidate advanced training in Engineering and Management, select curricular units aligned with their own research direction, and complete transferable-skills training relevant to the development of the doctorate.
 
The research component leads to the definition, development, writing, and public defence of an original thesis.
 
## Curricular Component
 
The curricular component corresponds to the **Common Core**, with **30 ECTS**.
 
According to the curricular structure registered with DGES, the 30 ECTS are distributed as follows:
 
| Block | Credits |
|---|---:|
| Specialisation Block | 12 to 24 ECTS |
| Transversal Skills Block | 6 ECTS |
| Free Options Block | 0 to 12 ECTS |
| **Total** | **30 ECTS** |
 
The list of optional curricular units is set each year by the bodies of IST and of the Schools of the University of Lisbon that are competent under the law and the statutes.
 
The programme includes a Common Core and different specialisations associated with the doctoral thesis.
 
| Path / Specialisation | Credits associated with the thesis |
|---|---:|
| Free Path | 210 ECTS |
| Operations, Logistics and Supply Chain Management | 210 ECTS |
| Operations Research and Business Analytics | 210 ECTS |
| Decision and Risk Analysis | 210 ECTS |
| Strategy, Innovation and Entrepreneurship | 210 ECTS |
| Health Engineering and Management | 210 ECTS |
| Sustainability in Engineering and Management | 210 ECTS |
 
Each student's specialisation is defined by the scientific and technological content developed in the curricular unit **Doctoral Thesis in Engineering and Management**, on the advice of the supervisors, the coordinator, and the programme's scientific committee.
 
The **Free Path** is a route without a specialisation. In this case, the student completes the curricular units of the Common Core and the curricular unit **Doctoral Thesis in Engineering and Management** in order to obtain the degree.
 
The Specialisation Block allows the student to deepen advanced training in fundamental areas of Engineering and Management.

The Transversal Skills Block corresponds to **6 ECTS**.
 
These curricular units may include training in skills relevant to the development of the doctorate, such as scientific writing, science communication, research methodologies, scientific project management, ethics, leadership, entrepreneurship, and other transversal skills.
 
Check the current offer each year through the Técnico Doctoral School.

## Thesis Monitoring Committee (CAT)
 
The Thesis Monitoring Committee (CAT) monitors the scientific development of the doctorate, together with the student, the supervisory team, and the programme coordination.
 
The role of the CAT is to support the regular monitoring of the research plan, assess the progress of the work, and identify any needs for scientific, methodological, or institutional adjustment.
 
### Establishing the CAT and Deadline (Article 14)
 
According to the General Doctoral Regulations, the following main rules apply:
 
- **Deadline:** The proposed composition of the CAT must be submitted to the coordinator of the doctoral programme **within 12 months of the date of official enrolment** (Article 14, paragraph 2).
- **Composition:**
  - The committee may have a **maximum of six members** (additional members are permitted only exceptionally and with proper justification).
  - It comprises the supervisory team plus at least two professors, researchers, or experts of recognised standing who are eligible to sit on the jury, provided that the supervisory members do not outnumber the remaining members.
- **Chair:** The CAT is chaired by the longest-serving IST member of the highest academic rank, excluding members of the supervisory team.
The composition, operation, and formal monitoring milestones should be confirmed against the regulations applicable to Técnico's doctoral programmes and the specific rules of the programme.

## Progress in Research Activities
 
Progress in research activities corresponds to the continuous development of the scientific work leading to the thesis.
 
This phase generally includes:
 
- definition and consolidation of the research topic
- critical literature review
- formulation of research questions
- methodological development
- collection, processing, or production of data, when applicable
- development of models, methods, analyses, or empirical studies
- production of scientific results
- presentation and discussion of results in seminars, conferences, or other scientific contexts
- preparation of scientific publications, when applicable
Progress should be monitored together with the supervisory team, the CAT, and the programme coordination.
 
## Writing the Thesis
 
Writing the thesis brings together and sets out in writing the research carried out during the doctorate.
 
The thesis must present an original scientific contribution in the area of Engineering and Management and demonstrate the capacity for autonomous research, methodological mastery, and a critical framing of the problem studied.
 
The specific structure of the thesis should be agreed with the supervisory team and follow the applicable Técnico standards.
 
## Thesis Defence
 
The thesis defence is the public examination and discussion of the research carried out.
 
Submission, assessment, appointment of the jury, scheduling of the public examination, and the remaining procedures follow the regulations applicable to Técnico doctorates and the rules of the Postgraduate Area.
