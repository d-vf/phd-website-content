# Research Areas

The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Management encompasses advanced research in Scientific Methods in Management (*Management Science*) and in decision-making, analysis, organizational, and management problems in technological, corporate, public, and social contexts.

The research associated with the program should be understood at three complementary levels:

1. the scientific-disciplinary areas of the Department of Engineering and Management
2. the research centers and branches that frame the scientific activity of the faculty
3. the specific topics of supervision, research, and advanced teaching associated with the program's faculty

## Scientific-disciplinary Areas of the Department

The Department of Engineering and Management is organized into two scientific-disciplinary areas:

- **Systems Engineering and Management**
- **Organization Engineering and Management**

These areas cover problems of strategy, planning, decision-making, operations, logistics, economics, innovation, sustainability, organization, and analysis of complex systems.

## Associated Research Centers and Branches

A large part of the scientific research at DEG is developed within the framework of **CEGIST — Centre for Management Studies of Instituto Superior Técnico** and **IN+ — Center for Innovation, Technology and Policy Research**.

### CEGIST — Centre for Management Studies of Instituto Superior Técnico

CEGIST organizes its activity into research branches covering decision methods, operations research, logistics, sustainability, strategy, entrepreneurship, and innovation.

| Research Branch | Scientific Focus |
|---|---|
| **DECISING — DECISion science and management engineerING** | Methods, models, and decision support tools in contexts with multiple actors, multiple objectives, uncertainty, evaluation, prioritization, resource allocation, risk, project, policy, and strategic choice. |
| **MOSAIC — systems modelling and Methods of Operations reSearch And analytICs** | Systems modeling, operations research, single-objective and multi-objective optimization, mathematical programming, algorithms, analytical tools, and applications to complex engineering and management problems. |
| **OpLog — Operations, Logistics and Supply Chain Management** | Operations management, logistics, supply chains, strategic, tactical, and operational planning, decision support in industry, services, healthcare, energy, transport, retail, waste, and social services. |
| **SUSTAINS — SUSTainable solutions and Assessment for cIrcular ecoNomy Systems** | Sustainability, circular economy, economic, environmental, and social assessment, life cycle analysis, sustainable supply chains, and support tools for sustainability implementation and monitoring. |
| **SEI — Strategy, Entrepreneurship and Innovation** | Strategy, entrepreneurship, innovation, industrial dynamics, labor markets, economic and regional development, human capital, technological change, and evaluation of science, industry, and higher education policies. |

### IN+ — Center for Innovation, Technology and Policy Research

IN+ is an interdisciplinary research center in innovation, technology, and policy development. In the context of DEG and DeGest, the connection to IN+ is particularly relevant in topics that cross technology management, innovation policy, sustainability, energy transition, urban systems, industry, knowledge, and economic development.

The center organizes its activity around laboratories and areas such as:

| Area / Laboratory | Possible Framework in DeGest |
|---|---|
| **Technology Management and Policy** | Technology management, innovation policy, science and technology policy, policy evaluation, and technological change processes. |
| **Industrial Ecology and Sustainability** | Sustainability, circular economy, industrial metabolism, environmental assessment, energy transition, and decarbonization. |
| **Thermofluids, Combustion and Energy Systems** | Energy, technological systems, innovation in energy systems, and the link between technological development, industry, and public policies. |
