# Overview
The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Management embodies a strategic option of IST. Students should be able to master knowledge in a variety of methods within the field of Management Science across a broad range of themes. The approaches adopted are applicable to decision and analysis problems in the fields of business management, public policy, and where the interaction between technology, economy and society is required.

# History
Technological development has generated a growing convergence between secondary, tertiary, and even primary sectors in the use of wide application technologies. Engineers with higher education who are able to combine technological knowledge, management scientific methods, and organizational factors, tend to play an increasingly important role in business development both in industry and in services.

# The PhD program in "Engineering and Management" is therefore justified and relevant.

# Objectives
The PhD degree in Engineering and Management aims to enable the students to perform independent scientific research of an internationally recognized quality in management science in the contexts of academia, government, and business.

# Designed For
The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Management aims to attract national and international students with a Licenciate (5 years) degree or a Masters degree in Engineering, Economics or Management.

# Employment
Those who have successfully completed the Doctoral Program in Engineering and Management may carry out their activity primarily in higher education institutions, research laboratories, companies and government agencies as well as in public administration.

# Operational Regime
The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Management has two components: A curricular part, which involves a set of courses, with 30 ECTS in total, and a PhD thesis that should involve original research. The courses will be taught in English, if there are foreign students attending.

# Tuition
Each year, the Board of IST defines the fees for the various programs of education at IST. This information is available at the Post-Graduate and Vocational Training Unit (NPGFC).
