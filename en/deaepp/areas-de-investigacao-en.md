# Research Areas

The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Public Policy (DEAEPP) encompasses advanced research at the interface between engineering, technology, and public policy, focusing on the design, analysis, and evaluation of policies in domains where technology is a determining factor, and on decision support for complex, unstructured, and large-scale problems.

The program was developed in partnership with the Department of Engineering and Public Policy at Carnegie Mellon University and is part of the **CMU-Portugal** program. The predominant scientific area of the cycle of studies is **Engineering and Management**.

The research associated with the program should be understood at three complementary levels:

1. the scientific-disciplinary areas of the Department of Engineering and Management
2. the research units that frame the scientific activity of the faculty
3. the specific topics of supervision, research, and advanced teaching associated with the program's faculty

## Scientific-disciplinary Areas of the Department

The Doctoral Program in Engineering and Public Policy is framed by the Department of Engineering and Management, which is organized into two scientific-disciplinary areas:

- **Systems Engineering and Management** (EGS)
- **Organization Engineering and Management** (EGO)

In the EPP, these areas are reflected in the curricular component itself — which includes EGO curricular units (Theory, Practice and Analysis of Public Policies; Quantitative Research Methods) and EGS units (Models and Applications in Decision Analysis) — and cover decision problems, policy analysis, economics, innovation, energy, sustainability, information systems, and analysis of complex systems relevant to public policies.

### CEGIST — Centre for Management Studies of Instituto Superior Técnico

CEGIST organizes its activity into research branches covering decision methods, operations research, strategy, entrepreneurship, innovation, and sustainability. In the context of the EPP, the branches linked to decision science and to strategy, entrepreneurship, and innovation are particularly relevant.

| Research Branch | Scientific Focus |
|---|---|
| **DECISING — DECISion science and management engineerING** | Methods, models, and decision support tools in contexts with multiple actors, multiple objectives, uncertainty, evaluation, prioritization, resource allocation, risk, policy, and strategic choice. |
| **SEI — Strategy, Entrepreneurship and Innovation** | Strategy, entrepreneurship, innovation, industrial dynamics, labor markets, economic and regional development, human capital, technological change, and evaluation of science, industry, and higher education policies. |
| **MOSAIC — systems modelling and Methods of Operations reSearch And analytICs** | Systems modeling, operations research, optimization, and analytical tools applied to complex engineering and management problems. |
| **SUSTAINS — SUSTainable solutions and Assessment for cIrcular ecoNomy Systems** | Sustainability, circular economy, economic, environmental, and social assessment, and support tools for sustainability implementation and monitoring. |

### LARSyS — Laboratory of Robotics and Engineering Systems

LARSyS is an Associate Laboratory dedicated to research in complex and socio-technical engineering systems, bringing together four units (ISR-Lisboa, **IN+**, ITI, and MARETEC). In the context of the EPP, the most relevant connection is through **IN+ — Center for Innovation, Technology and Policy Research**, in topics that cross technology management, innovation policy, energy systems, sustainability, mobility, and decarbonization.

| Area / Unit | Possible Framework in EPP |
|---|---|
| **Technology Management and Policy (IN+)** | Technology management, innovation policy, science and technology policy, policy evaluation, and technological change processes. |
| **Energy Systems (IN+)** | Planning and management of energy systems, energy transition, energy and climate, and the link between technology, industry, and public policies. |
| **Industrial Ecology and Sustainability (IN+)** | Sustainability, circular economy, environmental assessment, and decarbonization. |

### INESC INOV — Instituto de Engenharia de Sistemas e Computadores Inovação

INOV is a Technology and Innovation Center dedicated to applied research and technology transfer in the field of ICTE, with areas such as artificial intelligence, data science, cybersecurity, sensing and IoT, cyber-physical systems, and digital transformation. In the context of the EPP, the connection is relevant in topics of information systems, IT governance, and digital transformation applied to organizational and public policy contexts.

## Thesis Topics and Previous Dissertations

The definition of new thesis topics should consider the active research units, the faculty's interests, and the dissertations already completed in the program. 

Recent EPP theses cover, among other topics:

- workforce demographics, transitions to entrepreneurship, and startup performance
- the role of culture in technology diffusion (mobile communications)
- science and society policies, public participation in science, and responsible research and innovation (RRI)
- prospects for offshore wind energy deployment
- varieties of national protectionism (firm entry, adaptive regulation, and technological differences in global shocks)
- sustainable energy-based rural electrification in developing countries

These topics reflect the articulation between technology, economics, environment, and public decision-making that characterizes the program, frequently co-supervised with Carnegie Mellon University within the scope of CMU-Portugal.
