# Brief Introduction
 
The DEAEPP is a doctoral programme that aims to train experts in public policy and technology management through research on subjects where technological, economic, and legal issues carry equal weight. The programme began with specialisations in the telecommunications and energy sectors and has since broadened to keep pace with continuous technological change. It provides strong analytical training to support informed decision-making on complex real-world problems.
 
# History
 
The DEAEPP is a doctoral programme developed in partnership with the Department of Engineering and Public Policy at Carnegie Mellon University in Pittsburgh, United States of America, a world leader in the field of public policy. It was created to address a national shortage of analysts and experts in complex, unstructured, large-scale problems — people able to produce the information and knowledge needed to manage and design public policy.
 
# Objectives
 
The objective of the DEAEPP is to train experts in the design of public technology policy addressing major development problems in areas where technology is a decisive factor, and in sectors marked by fragmented regulation, universal-service obligations, sensor and social networks, and climate change.
 
# Graduate Profile
 
Graduates of the DEAEPP go on to leadership roles and to the management of multidisciplinary teams working on projects in which engineering and technology are central but which also draw on knowledge from several other fields, of the kind the programme provides.
 
# Career Prospects
 
DEAEPP graduates have broad and varied employment prospects internationally. Historically, roughly one-third find employment in industry, managing interdisciplinary teams; one-third in government institutions and think tanks; and one-third pursue an academic career at leading international universities.
 
# Modes of Delivery
 
The programme operates in two formats:
 
1. In partnership with Carnegie Mellon University, where students spend half of the programme at CMU;
2. A programme delivered entirely by IST.
# Tuition Fees
 
Students enrolled full-time at both IST and CMU pay tuition fees at both institutions. Students who complete the programme entirely at IST pay tuition fees only at IST.
 
The IST Management Board sets the tuition fees for its educational programmes each year. This information is published in the IST Doctoral Tuition Fees Regulation.
