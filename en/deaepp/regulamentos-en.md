# Regulations and Official Documentation
 
This page gathers links to regulations, institutional documentation, and official information relevant to the Doctoral Programme in Engineering and Public Policy.
 
Current administrative information should always be confirmed with the Postgraduate Area and on the official Técnico pages. Students on the **IST/CMU dual degree** (CMU-Portugal Program) are also subject to the regulations applicable at Carnegie Mellon University.

## Tuition and Fees
 
Tuition, fees, and student insurance are set and published by Técnico; check the official page of the Postgraduate Area. On the dual degree, students enrolled full-time at both IST and CMU pay tuition fees at both institutions; students who take the whole programme at IST pay fees only at IST.
 
- [Regulations and tuition fees for doctoral programmes](https://posgraduacao.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/en/programas-doutorais/regulamentos/)
