# Regulations and Official Documentation

This page gathers links to regulations, institutional documentation, and official information relevant to the Doctoral Program in Engineering and Public Policy.

The current administrative information must always be confirmed with the Postgraduate Area and on the official Técnico pages. Students in the **IST/CMU dual degree** regime (CMU-Portugal program) are also subject to the regulations applicable at Carnegie Mellon University.

## Tuition and Fees

Tuition, fees, and school insurance are set and published by Técnico. Consultation should be done on the official page of the Postgraduate Area. In the dual degree regime, when the student is enrolled full-time at IST and CMU, tuition fees are payable at both institutions; when the path is entirely at IST, fees are only payable at this institution.

- [Regulations and tuition fees for doctoral programs](https://posgraduacao.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/programas-doutorais/regulamentos/)
