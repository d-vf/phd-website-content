## Applications, Enrollment, and Academic Processes

Técnico Postgraduate Area:

- Email: posgraduacao@tecnico.ulisboa.pt
- [Doctoral Programs — Postgraduate Area](https://posgraduacao.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/programas-doutorais/)
- [Forms](https://posgraduacao.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/programas-doutorais/formularios/)
- [Regulations](https://posgraduacao.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/programas-doutorais/regulamentos/)

In the case of the **IST/CMU dual degree** (CMU-Portugal program), candidates must also apply directly to the Engineering and Public Policy (EPP) program at Carnegie Mellon University and typically present recent results from the GRE and TOEFL exams. Admission depends on both institutions.

- [Carnegie Mellon University — Engineering and Public Policy (EPP)](https://epp.engineering.cmu.edu/)
- [Access regime on Fénix](https://fenix.tecnico.ulisboa.pt/cursos/deaepp/regime-de-acesso)
